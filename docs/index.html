The Reality of Practicing Law with AI

Consumer chatbots sound confident, but they aren’t lawyers. LexHarness builds in the practical checks and safeguards that real legal representation demands.

📄

Record Grounding

Every factual assertion anchors directly to your case exhibits with exact Bates numbers, transcript page-and-lines, and timestamps.

🔍

Second-Chair Review

An automated Citation Auditor checks authorities and state precedent before drafts are presented, flagging unsupported claims.

⚔️

Opposing Perspective

Anticipates procedural traps, waiver risks, and counterarguments from opposing counsel and the bench so you can cure them early.

🔒

Local-First Privilege

Your case files and drafts stay on your machine. Includes automatic client-name scrubbing before external API calls.
