Why Repurpose Coding Agents for Legal Practice?

Consumer chatbots sound fluent, but they guess from memory. Software engineering coding agents are built to search files, verify line-by-line citations, and run multi-step verification tools—making them ideal for legal work once governed by legal system prompts.

📄

Bates Record Grounding

Every factual assertion anchors directly to your case exhibits with exact Bates numbers (STATE-0001, STATE-BWC-0009), deposition page-and-lines, and timestamps.

🔍

Citation & Record Auditor

An automated second-chair reviewer checks every factual and statutory claim against your exhibits and CourtListener precedent before you see the draft.

⚔️

Opposing Counsel Review

Anticipates procedural traps, waiver risks, and counterarguments from opposing counsel and the bench so weaknesses are cured in redline before filing.

🔒

Privileged PII Shield

Your database stays on your machine. Client names, SSNs, DOBs, and addresses are scrubbed locally before cloud dispatch while preserving Bates stamps.
